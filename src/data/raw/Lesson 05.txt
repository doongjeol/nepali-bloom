2. PART I: DIALOGUE (대화문)
--------------------------------------------------
(a) 쇼핑 가기
1. नमस्ते । / नमस्ते ।
   (namaste.! / namaste.)
   Hello.! / Hello.

2. कहाँ जाने ? / बजार जाने ।
   (kahãã jaane ? / bajaar jaane.)
   Where are you going ? / I'm going to the bazaar.

3. बजारमा किन जाने ? / तरकारी किन्न जाने ।
   (bajaarmaa kina jaane ? / tarkaari kinna jaane.)
   Why are you going to the bazaar ? / I'm going to buy vegetables.

4. अनि कहाँ जाने ? / डेरामा फर्कने ।
   (ani kahãã jaane ? / Deraamaa pharkane.)
   Where are you going then ? / I'm going to go back to my place.

5. कति बजे फर्कने ? / पाँच बजे होला ।
   (kati baje pharkane ? / pããch baje holaa.)
   What time are you heading back ? / Probably, at five o'clock.

(b) 가게에서
1. yahãã suntalaa chha ? / chha.
   (yahãã suntalaa chha ?) / (chha.)
   Do you have oranges ? / Yes, I do.

2. ek kiloko kati ? / tis rupiyãã.
   (ek kiloko kati ?) / (tis rupiyãã.)
   How much for a kilo ? / Thirty.

3. mahango bhayo. pachchismaa dinos. / hunchha. kati kilo line ?
   (mahango bhayo. pachchismaa dinos.) / (hunchha. kati kilo line ?)
   They're expensive. I'll give you twenty five rupees. / O.K. How many kilos do you want ?

4. dui kilo line. / la, linos.
   (dui kilo line.) / (la, linos.)
   I'll take two kilos / Here, take them.


(c)

1. asan Tolmaa phalphul paainchha ? / paainchha.
   (asan Tolmaa phalphul paainchha ?) / (paainchha.)
   Are fruits available in Asan Tole ? / Yes, they are.

2. chij ni ? / chij ta paaïdaina.
   (chij ni ?) / (chij ta paaïdaina.)
   How about cheese ? / No, cheese is not available.

3. chij kahãã paainchha ta ? / niuroDmaa paainchha.
   (chij kahãã paainchha ta ?) / (niuroDmaa paainchha.)
   Where is cheese found then ? / It's found in New Road.

4. ek kiloko kati parchha ? / ek saya chaalis rupiyãã parchha.
   (ek kiloko kati parchha ?) / (ek saya chaalis rupiyãã parchha.)
   How much is a kilo ? / It costs 140 rupees.

(d)

1. e saahuji ! phul chha ? / chha. katiwaTaa line ?
   (e saahuji ! phul chha ?) / (chha. katiwaTaa line ?)
   Oh shopkeeper ! Do you have eggs ? / Yes, I do. How many would you like ?

2. euTaako kati ho ? / pããch mohor.
   (euTaako kati ho ?) / (pããch mohor.)
   How much are they a piece ? / Two and a half rupees.

3. chaarwaTaa dinos. / linos.
   (chaarwaTaa dinos.) / (linos.)
   Give me four, please. / Here, take them, please.

4. jammaa kati bhayo ? / das rupiyãã.
   (jammaa kati bhayo ?) / (das rupiyãã.)
   How much does it come to ? / Ten rupees.


4. GRAMMAR NOTES (문법 정리)
--------------------------------------------------
1. mahanago / sasto: 가격이 비싸고 쌈을 나타내는 형용사입니다.
2. miTar: 옷감 등을 잴 때 사용하는 단위입니다.
3. ali: '조금', '약간'이라는 뜻으로 형용사를 수식합니다. (예: ali sasto - 조금 더 싼)
4. jammaa: 합계를 나타낼 때 사용합니다.
5. bhaayo: '되었다'는 뜻으로, 합계 금액을 말할 때 "전부 ~원입니다(되었습니다)"의 맥락으로 쓰입니다.
6. -aa/-o ending adjectives: 네팔어 형용사 중 'o'나 'aa'로 끝나는 것들은 수식하는 명사의 성/수에 따라 변하기도 하지만, 구어체에서는 주로 기본형을 많이 사용합니다.
