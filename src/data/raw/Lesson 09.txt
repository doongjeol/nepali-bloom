1. VOCABULARY (어휘)
--------------------------------------------------
aba            : now (지금/이제) 
baalnu         : to light (불을 켜다) 
batti          : light (불/전등) 
chaalis        : forty (40) 
bidyaarthi     : student (학생) 
hernu          : to look (보다) 
hijo           : yesterday (어제) 
kaagati        : lemon (레몬) 
keraa          : banana (바나나) 
nibhaaunu      : to turn off (light) (불을 끄다) 
paaunu         : to get, to find (얻다/찾다) 
pachchis       : twenty-five (25) 
phohar         : dirty (더러운) 
saphaa         : clean (깨끗한) 
tis            : thirty (30) 


2. PART I: DIALOGUE (대화문)
--------------------------------------------------
(a) 개인 신상 및 나이
1. tapaaïko naam ke ho ? / mero naam Tim ho. 
2. tapaaïko ghar kahãã ho ? / mero ghar amerikaa ho. 
3. tapaat kun bhaashaa bolnuhunchha ? / ma angreji bolchhu. 
4. tapaaï ahile kun bhaashaa sikdai hunuhunchha ? / ma nepaali sikdaichhu. 
5. tapaaï kati barsha hunubhayo ? / ma bis barsha bhae. 
   (당신은 몇 살입니까? / 저는 20살이 되었습니다/20살입니다.) 

(b) 제3자 소개 (평화봉사단원)
1. wahaa kun bhaashaa bolnuhunchha ? / wahaa angreji bolnuhunchha. 
2. wahaa ke kaam garnuhunchha ? / wahaa pis kor swayam sewak hunuhunchha. 
   (그분은 무슨 일을 합니까? / 그분은 평화봉사단(Peace Corps) 자원봉사자입니다.) 
3. wahaa kati barsha hunubhayo ? / wahãã baais barsha hunubhayo. 
   (그분은 몇 살입니까? / 그분은 22살입니다.) 
4. wahaa kahãã basnuhunchha ? / wahãã paaTanmaa basnuhunchha. 
   (그분은 어디에 삽니까? / 그분은 파탄(Patan)에 삽니다.)
