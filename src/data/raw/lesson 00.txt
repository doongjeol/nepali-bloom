
AN INTRODUCTION TO THE NEPALI SOUND SYSTEM

The Nepali alphabet is arranged in the following order. To use a Nepali-English Dictionary, this order must be memorized. The Roman letters used in this book are given with their equivalents in Devanagari script.

VOWELS
अ (a), आ (aa), इ (i), ई (i), उ (u), ऊ (u), ए (e), ऐ (ai), ओ (o), औ (au)

CONSONANTS
क (k), ख (kh), ग (g), घ (gh), ङ (ng)
च (ch), छ (chh), ज (j), झ (jh), ञ (n)
ट (T), ठ (Th), ड (D), ढ (Dh), ण (n)
त (t), थ (th), द (d), ध (dh), न (n)
प (p), फ (ph), ब (b), भ (bh), म (m)
य (y), र (r), ल (l), व (w), श (sh)
ष (sh), स (s), ह (h), क्ष (ksh), त्र (tra)
ज्ञ (gyn)

NOTE:
1) The vowels i and u have two forms in written Nepali: short form (इ, उ) and long form (ई, ऊ). However, since no distinction is made in spoken Nepali, they will be treated as the same in this book.
2) There are three different "s" sounds in written Nepali (श, ष, स). In common spoken Nepali, they are all pronounced as 'स'.
3) All Nepali vowel sounds have nasalized forms. Tilde (~) has been used in this book to nasalize a sound.

A BRIEF GUIDE TO THE PRODUCTION OF THE NEPALI SOUNDS

VOWELS
1. अ (a) - Short, mid-central vowel similar to 'about'.
   - अब (aba) - now
   - असल (asal) - good
   - दस (das) - ten
   - उठ (uTha) - stand up!

2. आ (aa) - Long, low, unsounded vowel similar to 'father'.
   - आमा (aamaa) - mother
   - आगो (aago) - fire
   - न आउ (na aau) - don't come!
   - जरा (jaraa) - root

3. इ (i) - Short, high front, unrounded vowel similar to 'fit'.
   - इनार (inaar) - well
   - इन्द्र (indra) - the god of rain
   - गीत (git) - song
   - पनि (pani) - also

4. ई (i) - Long but produced the same way (i) is.
   - ईश्वर (ishwar) - God
   - पानी (paani) - water
   - दिदी (didi) - elder sister
   - बहिनी (bahini) - younger sister
   - तीर (tir) - arrow
   - तिर (tira) - towards

5. उ (u) - Short, high back, rounded vowel similar to 'put'.
   - उठ (uTha) - stand up!
   - बाउन्न (baaunna) - fifty-two
   - जाउ (jaau) - go!

6. ऊ (u) - Long but produced the same way as (u).
   - ऊन (un) - wool
   - ठूलो (Thulo) - big
   - फूल (phul) - flower

7. ए (e) - Long, high-mid, front, unrounded vowel.
   - एक (ek) - one
   - गए (gae) - they went
   - खाएकोछु (khaaeko chhu) - I have eaten.

8. ऐ (ai) - Low-mid, front, unrounded diphthong (a + i).
   - ऐना (aina) - looking glass
   - सबै (sabai) - all
   - आजै (aajai) - today (emphatic)
   - बजै (bajai) - grandmother
   - मकै (makai) - corn
   - ऐठन (aiThan) - nightmare

9. ओ (o) - Long, high-mid, back rounded vowel.
   - ओत (ot) - shelter
   - फोहोर (phohor) - dirty
   - बाटो (baaTo) - road

10. औ (au) - Long, low-mid, back and rounded diphthong (अ + उ).
    - औलो (aulo) - malaria
    - थियौ (thiyau) - you were
    - जौ (jau) - barley
    - औषधी (ausadhi) - medicine
    - कौवा (kauwaa) - crow

CONSONANTS
Nepali has 33 consonant sounds divided into five broad categories.

1. Velar Consonants ( articulated at the soft palate/velum)
   1.1 क (k) - Unvoiced, non-aspirate. Ex: कलम (kalam) - pen, नक्कल (nakkal) - copy, छाक (chhaak) - food for one meal.
   1.2 ख (kh) - Aspirated form of 'k'. Ex: खानु (khaanu) - to eat, पर्खनु (parkhanu) - to wait, लेख (lekh) - article.
   1.3 ग (g) - Voiced counterpart of (k). Ex: गन (gana) - count!, पागल (paagal) - insane, काग (kaag) - crow.
   1.4 घ (gh) - Voiced counterpart of 'kh'. Ex: घर (ghar) - house, बाघ (baagh) - tiger, उघार्नु (ughaarnu) - to open.
   1.5 ङ (ng) - Voiced and nasal. Ex: नङ (nang) - finger or toe nail, टाङ्गो (Taango) - a long stick, रङ (rang) - color.

2. Palatal Consonants (articulated at the hard palate)
   2.1 च (ch) - Unvoiced, non-aspirated. Similar to 'ch' in 'church'. Ex: चार (chaar) - four, बचन (bachan) - word, चम्चा (chamchaa) - spoon.
   2.2 छ (chh) - Voiceless aspirated counterpart of (ch). Ex: छ (chha) - six, कान्छी (kaanchhi) - youngest female, माछा (maachhaa) - fish.
   2.3 ज (j) - Voiced, unaspirated palatal sound. Ex: जरा (jaraa) - root, आज (aaja) - today, भजन (bhajan) - hymn.
   2.4 झ (jh) - Aspirated counterpart of (j). Ex: झूटो (jhuTho) - lie, बुझ्नु (bujhnu) - to understand, साझा (saajhaa) - partnership.
   2.5 ञ (n) - Nasalized palatal sound. Rarely used.

3. Retroflex Consonants (tongue arching backward in the oral cavity)
   3.1 ट (T) - Unvoiced, non-aspirate. Ex: टाउको (Taauko) - head, घटाउ (ghaTaau) - subtract, केटाकेटी (keTaakeTi) - children.
   3.2 ठ (Th) - Unvoiced aspirate. Ex: ठाउँ (Thaaũ) - place, कोठा (koThaa) - room, अठार (aThaara) - eighteen.
   3.3 ड (D) - Voiced, non-aspirate. Ex: डर (Dar) - fear, लडाई (laDaaĩ) - fight, हाडे (haaDe) - bone.
   3.4 ढ (Dh) - Voiced, aspirate. Ex: ढोका (Dhokaa) - door, बढार्नु (baDhaarnu) - to sweep, चढ (chaDha) - climb.
   3.5 ण (n) - Rarely used.

4. Dental Consonants (articulated at the teeth)
   4.1 त (t) - Unvoiced, non-aspirate. Ex: तास (taas) - playing card, बतास (bataas) - wind, सर्बत (sarbat) - cold drink.
   4.2 थ (th) - Unvoiced, aspirate. Ex: थाल (thaal) - plate, बथान (bathaan) - group, अनाथ (anaath) - orphan.
   4.3 द (d) - Voiced, non-aspirate. Ex: दाम (daam) - price, बिबाद (bibaad) - argument, बदाम (badaam) - peanut.
   4.4 ध (dh) - Voiced, aspirate. Ex: धर्म (dharma) - religion, आधा (aadhaa) - half, राजधानी (raajdhaani) - capital.
   4.5 न (n) - Nasal dental consonant. Ex: नाम (naam) - name, इनार (inaar) - well, पानी (paani) - water.

5. Bi-labial Consonants (articulated by both lips)
   5.1 प (p) - Unvoiced, non-aspirate. Ex: पानी (paani) - water, तपाईं (tapaaĩ) - you (Hon.), ताप (taap) - heat.
   5.2 फ (ph) - Unvoiced, aspirate. Ex: फोहोर (phohor) - dirty, फलफूल (phalphul) - fruits, जवाफ (jawaaph) - answer.
   5.3 ब (b) - Voiced, non-aspirate. Ex: बाटो (baaTo) - way, बराबर (baraabar) - equal, बा (baa) - father.
   5.4 भ (bh) - Voiced, aspirate. Ex: भित्र (bhitra) - inside, अभागी (abhaagi) - unlucky.
   5.5 म (m) - Bi-labial nasal sound. Ex: मलाई (malaai) - me, नाम (naam) - name, आमा (aamaa) - mother.

6. Remaining Consonant Sounds
   6.1 य (y) - Unrounded, palatal semi-vowel. Ex: यता (yataa) - hither, बयासी (bayaasi) - eighty-two, समय (samaya) - time.
   6.2 र (r) - Voiced, non-aspirate, rolled alveolar. Ex: राम्रो (raamro) - good/pretty, कराउ (karaau) - shout!
   6.3 ल (l) - Voiced, non-aspirate lateral sound. Ex: लामो (laamo) - long, कलम (kalam) - pen, दाल (daal) - lentil soup.
   6.4 व (w) - Bi-labial semi-vowel. Tendency to pronounce as 'b'. Ex: वारी (waari) - side of river, वरिपरि (waripari) - around.
   6.5 स (s) - Unvoiced dental sibilant. Ex: पचास (pachaas) - fifty, कसरी (kasari) - how.
   6.6 श, ष - In Nepali words, these 's' sounds are pronounced as स (s).
   6.7 ह (h) - Glottal fricative. Ex: हामी (haami) - we, मह (maha) - honey, शहर (shahar) - city, पोहोर (pohor) - last year.

7. Compound Consonant Sounds
   7.1 क्ष (ksh) - k + sh. Ex: अक्षर (akshar) - alphabet.
   7.2 त्र (tra) - t + r. Ex: भित्र (bhitra) - inside.
   7.3 ज्ञ (gyn/gyaan) - gy + n. Ex: ज्ञान (gyaan) - knowledge.
